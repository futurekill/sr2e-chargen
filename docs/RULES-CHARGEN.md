## The steps (core Priority system, p.44–47)

0. **Concept.** Decide what the character does and why; a magician decides hermetic/shamanic and, if shamanic, the
   totem now, because it guides later choices (p.44).
1. **Allocate priorities** A–E, each to exactly one of five categories: Race, Magic, Attributes, Skills, Resources
   (p.44). The Master Character Creation Table (p.47):

   | Priority | Race | Magic | Attributes | Skills | Resources |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | A | Metahuman | Human Magician | 30 points | 40 points | 1,000,000¥ / 50 Force |
   | B | Human | Human Adept / Metahuman Magician | 24 | 30 | 400,000¥ / 35 |
   | C | Human | Metahuman Adept | 20 | 24 | 90,000¥ / 25 |
   | D | Human | — | 17 | 20 | 5,000¥ / 15 |
   | E | Human | — | 15 | 17 | 500¥ / 5 |

2. **Race** (p.44). Metahuman (dwarf, elf, ork, troll) REQUIRES Priority A in Race; B–E = human. Racial
   Modifications Table (p.45): Dwarf +1 Body, −1 Quickness, +2 Strength, +1 Willpower, thermographic, disease
   resistance (+2 Body vs disease only); Elf +1 Quickness, +2 Charisma, low-light; Ork +3 Body, +2 Strength,
   −1 Charisma, −1 Intelligence, low-light; Troll +5 Body, −1 Quickness, +4 Strength, −2 Intelligence, −1 Willpower,
   −2 Charisma, thermographic, +1 Reach (armed/unarmed), dermal armour (+1 Body).
3. **Magic** (p.45). Full magician: human A, metahuman B. Adept: human B, metahuman C. Hermetic or shamanic is the
   player's choice, not the priority's.
4. **Attributes** (p.45). Points for the six Physical and Mental Attributes (Body, Quickness, Strength,
   Intelligence, Willpower, Charisma). Each at least 1; after racial modifiers, none above the Racial Maximum
   Table (p.43) and none below 1. Racial modifiers are applied AFTER allocation.
   - Special Attributes are not bought: **Essence** starts at 6 (cyberware reduces it); **Magic** starts at 6 for a
     magician, = Essence rounded down (Essence 3.75 → Magic 3); **Reaction** = (Quickness + Intelligence) ÷ 2,
     rounded down (p.45).
   - Racial Maximum Table (p.43), Human / Elf / Dwarf / Ork / Troll: Body 6/6/7/9/11; Quickness 6/7/5/6/5;
     Strength 6/6/8/8/10; Charisma 6/8/6/5/4; Intelligence 6/6/6/5/4; Willpower 6/6/7/6/5; Essence 6 all;
     Magic 6 all; Reaction 6/6/5/5/4.
5. **Skills** (p.45). Points: A 40, B 30, C 24, D 20, E 17. Any skills from the Skills chapter (p.66); Sorcery and
   Conjuring only with a Magic Rating. No starting skill above 6 unless a Concentration/Specialization; minimum 1.
   - Concentrations and Specializations (optional rule, p.45, details p.70): one per general skill; Concentration =
     skill +1 and the general skill −1; Specialization = skill +2, general −2, and the Concentration at the
     original rating (Firearms 5 → Firearms 3, Pistols 5, Ares Predator 7).
   - Special skills (p.74) with GM approval. **Language**: native language free at Intelligence + 2 (may exceed 6);
     a Street lifestyle also gives a local dialect (City Speak, Tunnel Talk) at ½ Intelligence, rounded down;
     other languages bought like skills (p.74).
6. **Resources** (p.46): nuyen for everyone, Force Points for magicians only.
   - **Cyberware**: list price (p.254); reduces Essence; Essence never below 0; Magic follows Essence. During
     creation ignore Availability, Street Price and surgery costs.
   - **Gear**: list price (pp.254–265); no starting gear rating above 6 (also p.43). Incidentals (ammunition, armour,
     decks, programs, conjuring materials, magical libraries) are bought here too.
   - **Foci**: the monetary cost (p.254) AND the Bonding cost in Force Points (p.137).
   - **Spells**: Force Points 1 per Force (from the Spell Directory pp.151–158); no starting spell above Force 6;
     keep totem modifiers in mind (p.119).
   - **Extras** (Cost of Extras table, p.46): contact 5,000¥; buddy 10,000¥ (only one at creation, p.43; an Archetype
     at ratings 4); gang/tribe 50,000¥ (2D6 members at 3s, p.44); followers 200,000¥ (five different Archetypes,
     1D6+1 at a time, p.44). **Two free contacts** for every character (p.43, p.46).
   - **Lifestyle** must be bought (else Street): Street 0¥, Squatter 100¥, Low 1,000¥, Middle 5,000¥, High 10,000¥,
     Luxury 100,000¥ per month; several months may be prepaid (p.46; lifestyles in detail p.189).
7. **Optional rules** (p.46):
   - **More Metahumans**: metahuman Race moves to Priority C; Magic becomes A for full magicians and B for adepts in
     ALL races.
   - **Allergies** (metahumans only, player's option): extra points per the Allergy and Severity Table (p.46;
     details p.220); the bonus from ONE category each for the allergy and for the severity (may differ):
     Uncommon (iron, silver) +1 Attribute / +1 Skill / +1 Force / +200¥; Common (plastic, pollutants, sunlight)
     +2 / +2 / +2 / +400¥; severity Nuisance —; Mild +1 / +2 / +1 / +100¥; Moderate +2 / +3 / +2 / +200¥;
     Severe +3 / +4 / +3 / +300¥.
8. **Completing the character** checklist (p.47): magician type/totem; Race and Magic priorities; all Attribute
   Points spent and racial modifiers applied; all Skill Points spent with Concentration/Specialization adjustments;
   equipment, cyberware, spells, contacts, foci; a lifestyle.
9. **Starting money and Karma** (p.47):
   - unspent Resource nuyen converts at **10:1** (15,000¥ left → 1,500¥);
   - every character also starts with **3D6 × 1,000¥** in any form;
   - Karma Pool: human 1, metahuman 2 (1 under More Metahumans); a new team's Team Karma Pool starts at 2, and
     players may contribute starting Karma to it (p.191).
10. **Fleshing out** (p.47–48): Twenty Questions (sex, size, colouring, …) — flavour for the builder's notes step;
    no mechanics.

## After creation: Karma and advancement (p.190–191; renders of PDF 199–200, offset +9 here)

- **Awards** (p.190): the GM awards Karma at the end of an adventure (staying alive, success, danger; individual
  awards for roleplaying etc.). 90% becomes **Good Karma** (long-term improvement), 10% goes to the **Karma Pool**.
  - Book conflict, already ruled in the system (`karmaPoolCapacity`, sr2e-rules.mjs): p.190 says "round off in favor
    of Good Karma", p.191 says the Pool gets one-tenth "(round up)". The pool's own section (p.191) wins; the
    creator uses the same function.
- **Attributes** (p.190): +1 costs Good Karma = the NEW rating (Strength 5 → 6 costs 6). Above the racial maximum
  only with GM approval, at DOUBLE cost (human Strength 6 → 7 costs 14); the GM should discourage going past
  1.5 × the racial maximum. Reduced attributes may be raised back. Reaction, Essence and Magic can NEVER be raised
  with Karma (Reaction follows Quickness/Intelligence).
- **Skills** (p.190–191), Good Karma Cost for Skills table: general skill 2 × new rating; Concentration
  1.5 × new rating; Specialization 1 × new rating; Language 1 × new rating. After creation, general skills,
  Concentrations and Specializations improve separately (raising the general skill doesn't move the Concentration).
  Example (Iris): Firearms 1 → 2 costs 4; Pistols 3 → 4 costs 6; Beretta 101T 5 → 6 costs 6.
  - A new Concentration starts from the general skill (Firearms 4 → Pistols 5 costs 5 × 1.5 = 8 — rounding up is
    the book's own example); a new Specialization from the Concentration, else the general skill
    (Roomsweeper 5 × 1 = 5). A brand-new skill costs 1 Good Karma for rating 1.
  - A hermetic needs a sorcery library rated at least the Sorcery rating being bought.
- **Karma Pool** (p.191): refreshes each encounter; re-roll failures (1, then +1 per further re-roll on the same
  test), avoid an Oops (1), buy extra dice (1 each, at most the dice in use excluding pools), buy successes
  (1 each, needs a natural success; spent permanently). Team Karma (p.191): each member contributes at least 1;
  leaving halves it; multiple teams cost extra per point. (Play-time rules; the manager tracks the pools.)
- **Buying a lifestyle permanently** (p.190): 100 months' upkeep; Middle or better can be sold back for 2D6 × 10% of
  the price (1D6 without a SIN or in a hurry).

## Alternate methods: Shadowrun Companion (FASA 7905), "Character Creation Options" p.19–20

_Renders of PDF 20–21 (book = PDF − 1 here). All optional, GM's choice; the Companion recommends the whole group use
the same method (p.19)._

- **Altering priorities** (p.19): GM tables, not fixed rules: Race (e.g. ordinary metahumans at B, Priority A for
  exotic races such as variants or shapeshifters, pp.36–44; per-race priorities; or no Race category); Magic (e.g.
  shamans at B in a tribal game); Attributes/Skills (raise or lower the starting points, or give initial Karma);
  Resources (change the amounts, a flat amount for everyone, approved-equipment lists, an Availability limit — 6 is
  suggested). **Builder support:** a GM "house table" editor that overrides the Master Character Creation Table per
  campaign, plus an equipment allow-list/Availability cap.
- **Altering rating limits** (p.20): the starting cap of 6 on Attributes and Skills may be lowered (4: generalists)
  or raised (8: specialists). **Builder:** a campaign setting, default 6.
- **Sum-to-10** (p.20): priorities become numbers A=4, B=3, C=2, D=1, E=0; any combination totalling ≤ 10 (e.g.
  4,3,2,1,0; 4,4,1,1,0; 3,3,3,1,0) — each category then gets the Master Table row of its letter.
- **Point-Based Design** (p.20–21): the GM sets a Building Points allotment (100 recommended). Building Points
  Table (p.20): Race — metahuman 10, human 0; Magic — magician 20 (30 Force Points), adept 15 (30 Force Points),
  physical adept 15 (Force Points = Magic Rating); Attributes — 1 Attribute Point per 2 Building Points (GM sets
  rating limits); Skills — 1 Skill Point per 1 Building Point; Resources — additional Force Points 2 per 1 Building
  Point; nuyen: 0¥ −5, 500¥ 0, 5,000¥ 5, 90,000¥ 10, 200,000¥ 15, 400,000¥ 20, 650,000¥ 25, 1,000,000¥ 30.
  Magically active characters automatically get 30 Force Points (not the Resources priority).
- **Point-based details** (p.21; render PDF 22):
  - Magic: magicians and non-physical adepts get 30 Force Points free; they may BUY more at 1 Building Point per 2
    Force Points, or SELL them at 2 Force Points per 1 Building Point. Physical adepts get no 30 Force Points (nothing
    to sell) and buy powers with Magic-Rating points as usual (core p.125). The GM may lower/raise the magic costs.
  - Skills: 1 Building Point per level up to the GM's cap; Concentrations and Specializations chosen after purchase
    cost nothing extra (Virgil: "Demolitions (Plastic Explosives) 3/5" costs 4 = the purchased rating).
  - Attributes: 2 Building Points per point (Virgil: Body 3 = 6, Intelligence 5 = 10).
  - Spells (GM option): Force Points may replace Karma to start as an initiate, or to summon/maintain an ally spirit.
  - Worked example (p.21, **test fixture**): Virgil the SEAL decker = 102 BP — Race human 0, Magic none 0, Attributes
    Body 3/6, Quickness 4/8, Strength 3/6, Charisma 1/2, Intelligence 5/10, Willpower 4/8; Skills Computer 6, Computer
    (B/R) 6, Electronics 5, Computer Theory 3, Stealth 4, Demolitions (Plastic Explosives) 3/5 = 4, Unarmed Combat
    (Cyber-implanted Weaponry) 3/5 = 4, Athletics 4, Firearms 4, Japanese 2; Resources 400,000¥ = 20. The GM may
    require a 2-point Flaw to get back to 100, or allow a small range of overage.

## Edges and Flaws (Companion p.21–35)

_Renders of PDF 22–36 (book = PDF − 1). The catalogue itself is content: the `sr2e-shadowrun-companion` module's
`sc-qualities` pack (86 items, `system.pointValue`, `kind` edge/flaw, `category`, `notes`). M0 checked every item's
point value against the Edges and Flaws Table (p.35, re-read at 300 dpi): all match except Mysterious
Cyberware/Bioware, which the module had at −5 and the book prints −3 (p.34 and p.35); fixed in module 0.4.1._

- **Optional and per-character** (p.21): usable with the priority system, Sum-to-10 or point-based; the GM may forbid
  any or all. GM option: allow adding them in play (the Companion recommends roleplaying them in instead).
- **Priority / Sum-to-10 games** (p.21–22): any combination whose point values total **exactly zero** (Ambidexterity 2
  + Color Blind −1 + Combat Monster −1).
- **Point-based games** (p.22): Edges cost Building Points equal to their value; Flaws ADD Building Points equal to
  their value (Blind −6 → +6 BP). The GM may also allow the zero-sum rule there.
- **Limitations** (p.22): an Edge never raises an Attribute, skill or Spell Force above the standard limits and
  racial maximums for new characters (GM approval excepted); a TN is never reduced below 2 by Edges/Flaws; Edges and
  Flaws can't be combined unless an entry says so (no Pacifist + Total Pacifist); the education Edges/Flaws (Illiterate,
  Uneducated, High School, Technical School, College) may be banned as a group by the GM.
- **Eliminating Flaws** (p.22, play-time, for the manager): GM-approved, through roleplaying, costs Good Karma = 10 ×
  the Flaw's point value (BTL addiction, −3: 30 Karma) plus any nuyen the GM sets for medical procedures.
- **Custom Edges/Flaws** (p.22): ±6 points at most, GM-approved (Stacy's "Good Looking and Knows It" 2 → 1). **Builder:**
  a GM custom-quality editor with that cap.
- **Ranged and conditional values** (p.35; the module stores the LOWEST value, the player sets the chosen one — the
  builder must offer the range and validate it):

| Entry | Points |
|---|---|
| High Pain Tolerance | Variable (Edge) |
| Lightning Reflexes | 2 to 6 |
| Will to Live | 1 to 3 |
| Day Job | 1 to 3 |
| Good Reputation | 1 or 2 |
| Bonus Force Point | 1 to 3 |
| Magic Resistance | 1 to 4 |
| Magical Talent: Summoning Talent | 3/5 |
| State-of-the-Art Model | 2, 4 or 6 |
| Allergy | −1 to −4 |
| Infirm | −1 to −5 |
| Amnesia | −2 to −5 |
| Phobia | −1 to −4 |
| Bad Reputation | −1 to −4 |
| Compulsive, Dependent | Variable |
| Hunted | −2, −4 or −6 |
| Addiction | −1 Strongly Addictive / −1 Debilitating / −2 Incapacitating (the table's sub-rows) |
| Bio-Rejection | −5 (−2 for magically active characters) |
| Blind | −6 (−2 for magically active characters) |
| Sensitive System | −3 (−2 for magically active characters) |

  **M1/M7 consequence:** the Companion items need a range/conditional field (or the creator a lookup keyed by the
  item's source UUID) before the builder can validate them; decide in M7's plan. Per-entry creation effects (Bonus
  Attribute/Skill/Force Point, Exceptional Attribute, Extra Contact, education, State-of-the-Art availability caps
  Availability 10 / 20 / delta-grade any, Registered Equipment needs a valid SIN) are read from each entry (p.23–34)
  when M7 builds them; the Foundry system already implements `attributeBonus`/`maximumBonus` (Quench `sr2e.qualities`).

## New character types (Companion p.36–44)

_Renders of PDF 37–45._

- **Shapeshifters** (p.36–38; already in the Foundry system, sr2e 0.125.0 + module 0.4.0): Race MUST be Priority A;
  the Shapeshifter Character Creation Table replaces the Master Table's other columns — B: Magician, 24 Attribute
  Points, 30 Skill Points, 400,000¥/35 Force Points; C: Adept, 20, 24, 90,000¥/25; D: —, 17, 20, 5,000¥/15; E: —, 15,
  17, 500¥/5. Base Attributes bought per standard rules, **max 6**; then the species' Human Form modifiers (final
  human-form ratings) and Animal Form modifiers (Shapeshifter Attribute Modifiers Table) — final ratings are NOT
  subject to racial maximums. **No free starting contacts**: contacts are bought from Resources. **No cyberware**
  (rejected on shifting). The Foundry system's `shapeshift-rules.mjs` holds the table; the creator reuses it (D1).
- **Metahuman variants** (p.38–44; content in the module's `sc-metatypes`, each = the base race's modifiers with the
  listed exceptions): no creation-priority change, EXCEPT **Oni** (p.42): at the GM's discretion a MAGICAL oni may take
  Resources A, Race B, Magic C (non-magical oni use the regular metahuman priorities). Every variant gets a **Hostile**
  reaction (core Social Skill Table p.182) in all initial social contacts with other metahumans (p.38). **Dryads**
  (p.44): a limited Animal Empathy Edge (birds and small tree-living animals) that need not be balanced by a Flaw.
- **Metahuman albinism** (p.38): any race; no priority change; the character MUST take at least one of Bio-Rejection,
  Color Blind, Low Pain Tolerance, Night Blindness or Sensitive System WITHOUT gaining its value (no Building Points,
  no balancing Edge); each further Flaw from that list gives its points normally; +1 Willpower on top of any other
  creation increase. **Builder:** an "Albino" toggle enforcing that.
