# Rulings

Where the books are ambiguous, conflict, or leave a gap, the app follows the ruling here. Each one names the pages,
the reading chosen, and why. The user (the GM) can overturn any of them. **R1–R4 confirmed by the GM on 2026-11-18.**

| # | Topic | Pages | Ruling | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Starting skill cap with a concentration | core p.45, p.70 | The cap of 6 applies to the **general** Skill Rating after p.70's reduction. A concentration or specialization may go above 6, so 7 points with a concentration (Firearms 6, Pistols 8) is legal; 7 points with no concentration (Firearms 7) is not. | p.45: "no starting character may have a Skill Rating of more than 6, unless Concentrations and Specializations are assigned". The general rating is the Skill Rating; the concentration and specialization ratings are the exception the sentence allows. Decided 2026-11-18 (M2b code review); confirmed by the GM 2026-11-18. |
| R2 | Which language is the free local dialect | core p.46 | Any language the player names may be marked as the free Street dialect; the app does not keep a list of dialects. | The book gives examples ("City Speak or Tunnel Talk"), not a list; which dialect is local is the GM's call. |
| R3 | Magic priority buys exactly one type | core p.45–46 | The Magic priority buys the table's type exactly: a human magician A, a human adept B, a metahuman magician B, a metahuman adept C (More Metahumans: A magician, B adept). A higher letter does not buy the lower type. | p.45: "Human adepts must assign Priority B to Magic, and metahuman adepts must assign Priority C". |
| R4 | Improved Physical Attributes tier | core p.125 | Each +1 is priced by the attribute's NATURAL rating (bought + racial modifier) plus the improvement so far, against the racial maximum. Cyberware, bioware and spells do not move the tier. One power per attribute. | p.125 prices by "how much higher (or lower) than the character's Racial Maximum is the final attribute rating"; the racial maximum is a natural-rating limit, so the natural rating is the one compared. Decided 2026-11-18 (M2c-2 plan review); confirmed by the GM 2026-11-18. |
| R5 | Weapon focus: size and the weapon | core p.137, p.147, p.263 | The bond costs 4 × Force for a small weapon focus and 5 × Force for a large one. A weapon is **small when its Concealability is above 5**; Concealability 5 or less, or a weapon that cannot be concealed (99 in the data), is large. The focus bonds to one melee weapon the character buys (one focus per weapon, quantity 1), and its price is p.263's [(Reach + 1) × 100,000¥] + Force × 90,000¥ from that weapon's Reach. The weapon's own list price is paid too, as Foundry does. Only magicians bond a focus at creation (Force Points, p.46); a physical adept's weapon focus is an open question. | p.137's Focus Bonding Table lists "Weapon Focus, Small" and "Weapon Focus, Large" with no definition; how easy the weapon is to conceal is the GM's measure of its size. p.147 limits weapon foci to melee weapons. Size rule set by the GM 2026-11-18. |
